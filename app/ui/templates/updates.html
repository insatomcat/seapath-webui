Machine Kernel Pending Updated

An update runs apt-get dist-upgrade, one machine at a time, and reboots a machine only when it gets a new kernel, then checks the machines it updated. The root volume is snapshotted first, and a machine that fails to boot its new system is rolled back to it. A cluster member that reboots is put in standby first, so its guests move to the other members. Without a reboot the guests stay where they are, and when the upgrade restarts Pacemaker or Corosync, the cluster is put in maintenance for its length so that no guest is stopped or moved.
