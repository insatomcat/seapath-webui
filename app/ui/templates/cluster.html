{# Resources: what is running, and where. In SEAPATH most of them are VMs, one Pacemaker resource per guest. #}

Pacemaker decides where a resource runs, and this reports the decision. A failure count is cleared and a resource is moved with crm resource on the machine, or from Cockpit: this service never runs a cluster command, which is what keeps the inventory the only thing that configures a machine.
